Notes: when BGA shows only one checker move for a roll (the other die could not be played, or the game ended after bearing off the last checker), the unused die's value cannot be recovered from the log and is guessed — this is flagged in the messages panel. Bear-off "overage" (e.g. bearing off a 3-point with a 5 because no higher points are occupied) is detected by simulating the board, so doubles that mix exact and overage bear-offs (like 4/off 4/off 3/off 3/off for a rolled double-4) are reconstructed correctly. Taking a double is recognised from BGA's "accepts the cube" line; BGA's log doesn't record the double offer itself or a drop directly, so both are inferred from a gap in the move numbering right before a "wins the game" line (as opposed to a forfeit, which is stated explicitly, or a normal bear-off win, which follows straight on from the winning move). The match length isn't present in the log either, so it is guessed from the final score reached and can be overridden using the "Match length" field before converting again.
