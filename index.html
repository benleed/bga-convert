Notes: cube actions (double/take/drop) and resignations are not present in this BGA log format and are not handled. When BGA shows only one checker move for a roll (the other die could not be played, or the game ended after bearing off the last checker), the unused die's value cannot be recovered from the log and is guessed — this is flagged in the messages panel. Bear-off "overage" (e.g. bearing off a 3-point with a 5 because no higher points are occupied) is detected by simulating the board, so doubles that mix exact and overage bear-offs (like 4/off 4/off 3/off 3/off for a rolled double-4) are reconstructed correctly.
